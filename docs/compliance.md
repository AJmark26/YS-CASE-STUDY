## Real-property hazards (constraints section)

| Hazard | Handling | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Mirrors and glass | LiDAR confidence below 2 is discarded and a 2 cm voxel needs 3 hits, which removes ghosts seen from one angle only; a mirror seen for long still produces a phantom room behind the wall (known failure mode); the protocol says to avoid lingering on them | `ysplan/fuse.py`, `docs/capture_protocol.md` |
| Wet-look and dark surfaces | low-confidence depth is discarded; the damage detector only marks pixels, metric area comes from the geometry | `ysplan/fuse.py`, `ysplan/damage.py` |
| Low light | ARKit relocalisation shows as a pose jump; jumps cut the drift chunks and are reported in `drift.pose_jumps`; the protocol asks for lights on | `ysplan/drift.py`, `docs/capture_protocol.md` |
